# Accessibility

<!-- Include a brief statement describing why accessibility matters to your project, who should be able to use and contribute to it, and what this document covers (such as your accessibility commitments, contributor expectations, and issue-reporting process). -->

Accessibility is a core priority for my agenda app. An agenda is only useful if you can actually use it, and that includes people with disabilities and
people who rely on assistive technology such as screen readers, keyboard navigation, and voice control. I want everyone to be able to plan their days,
manage their events and tasks, and contribute to this project.

This document explains my accessibility commitment, what I expect from contributors to help uphold it, and how to report accessibility issues.
